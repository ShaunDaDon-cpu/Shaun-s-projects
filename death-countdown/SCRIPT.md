# DEATH COUNTDOWN

*A Roblox-style storyteller short. Vertical 9:16, 1:27.*

**CAST:** MASON (narrator, 12), MOM, DAD, SISTER, GRANDMA, THE COLLECTORS (three tall men in black suits with phone screens for faces)

**FORMAT NOTES:** Narration is in plain text. Character lines are labelled. Captions are one word at a time, yellow for the narrator and colour-tagged for the others (MOM pink, DAD blue, SISTER green, GRANDMA purple).

---

### ACT ONE — THE RULE

**0:00 · EXT. TOWN — DAY** *(crane down)*
A bright block town: big two-storey houses, giant trees, birds in V formation, drifting clouds. Everyone on the sidewalks walks staring at a glowing phone.
> In my town, phones show when you die.

**0:02 · CLOSE ON A PHONE**
A countdown ticking: **61y 4d 03:12:09**.
> A countdown. To the second.

**0:04 · EXT. SIDEWALK**
A spiky-haired show-off with sunglasses pushed up on his head flexes, with gold sparkles around him. His phone faces us: **80y 0d**. Neighbours gawk.
> People with eighty years left act like gods.

**0:06 · EXT. BUS STOP**
An old man waves goodbye to everyone like a maniac. His phone reads **00:00:12 — SAY BYE!**
> People with twelve seconds left say goodbye fast.

**0:09 · EXT. SKY**
A guy in a red shirt free-falls past clouds, grinning, his phone showing **40y**. The plane shrinks above him.
> One guy had forty years left, so he skydived with no parachute.

**0:13 · EXT. STREET — CRATER**
*BOOM.* A crater in the road, dust everywhere. He lies face-down in it and slowly gives a thumbs-up. Neighbours stare, unimpressed.
> He's fine. It's honestly annoying.

---

### ACT TWO — THE BOY WITH ZERO

**0:15 · EXT. SIDEWALK** *(colour drains)*
Mason looks down, worried. Cut to his point of view: his phone reads **0y 0d 00:00:00**.
*MUSIC DROPS to a single low note.*
> My phone says zero.

**0:17 · FLASHBACK — EXT. FOREST ROAD — NIGHT** *(faded, raining)*
"3 YEARS AGO." A wrecked school bus on its side with a crushed nose, shattered windows and a torn-off wheel. Smoke and flickering fire. Skid marks, potholes, glass, a smashed guardrail, a pine forest, police lights.
> It's said zero for three years. Since the bus crash.

**0:20 · INT. SCHOOL CAFETERIA — DAY**
Posters: EAT YOUR VEGGIES!, GOT MILK?. The LUNCH LADY carries a full tray to an empty table, sets it down and hurries away, glancing back nervously. Only then does Mason walk in. He stares at the waiting tray.
> The lunch lady sets out my tray before I even get there.

**0:23 · INT. CLASSROOM — DAY**
A "WELCOME TO 5TH GRADE!" banner. The chalkboard reads ATTENDANCE: Ava, Ben, Cleo, **Mason**, Dev, Ella. The TEACHER's speech bubbles go "Ava?", "Ben?", "Cleo?", then "Dev?" as checkmarks appear. A red chalk arrow jumps over Mason's name: "skipped?". Mason, front row, raises his hand. Nobody sees.
> My teacher skips my name at attendance.

**0:25 · INT. MASON'S BEDROOM — NIGHT**
A gamer kid's room: SPACE RACER 3 and PIXEL KNIGHTS posters, a glowing monitor, a red gaming chair, LED strips and string lights. Mason lies curled on his side under the blanket. MOM stands behind him in tears, fingers on his wrist. He sneaks one eye open.
> And every night, Mom checks my pulse when she thinks I'm asleep.

**0:29 · OVERHEAD — MASON IN BED**
The camera rotates above him. His face changes from worry to shock.
> So I figured it out. I'm a ghost.

---

### ACT THREE — THE COLLECTORS

**0:31 · CLOSE ON ALARM CLOCK**
**2:59** flips to **3:00 AM**.
*KNOCK. KNOCK. KNOCK.*
> Then at three A M, someone knocked.

**0:34 · DOORBELL CAM — "● REC  FRONT DOOR  03:00:14 AM"**
Three tall, thin men in black suits stand on the porch, their faces static screens with a grinning face flickering through. The night street glows behind them. The middle one slowly cranks his head sideways in creaking, stuttering jerks, then twitches. *CRACK.*
> Three men in black. Their faces were phone screens.

**0:36 · EXT. PORCH**
The camera pans across their full bodies. One by one, the screens glitch to **Mason's face**, blood-red with hollow eyes, over a flashing **OVERDUE** banner. Push in.
> Every screen showed my face, and one word. Overdue.

**0:40 · INT. LIVING ROOM — NIGHT**
The front door is shut and already cracking. DAD sprints across the room and throws his back against it, arms spread. *BANG! BANG! BANG!* The door jolts with every hit.
> **DAD:** Nobody opens that door!

**0:41 · INT. STAIRS** *(dolly zoom)*
Mason freezes on the stairs, his shock turning to hurt. *BANG.*
> I wasn't a ghost. I was a mistake.

**0:43 · INT. LIVING ROOM**
MOM kneels in front of Mason, real tears rolling down her face. Behind her, Dad still holds the door as it shudders. *BANG.*
> **MOM:** You were supposed to die in that crash. You didn't.

**0:46 · EXT. STREET — NIGHT** *(crane up)*
Porch lights click on down the whole street, one house after another. Neighbours step out with flashlights.
> **MOM (V.O.):** The whole town's been hiding you from the Collectors.

---

### ACT FOUR — THE FAMILY FIGHTS BACK

**0:49 · INT. LIVING ROOM**
Dad is braced against the bulging door. *BOOM!* It blasts off its hinges behind him and flies over his head in a storm of splinters. Dad is thrown to the floor. A Collector ducks through the empty doorway and leans its head into the house, cocked sideways, with one claw gripping the frame.
> The door came off its hinges.

**0:50 · INT. LIVING ROOM**
MOM winds up a frying pan. *BONK!* The Collector's screen cracks into a red, glitching web.
> Mom hit the first one so hard his screen cracked.

**0:53 · INT. LIVING ROOM** *(family photos, plants, a TV left on)*
Dad charges, wraps both arms around the second Collector, and they topple together. *CRASH!* Straight through the couch.
> Dad tackled the second one through the couch.

**0:55 · INT. KITCHEN**
SISTER stands on the counter waving both arms, then hurls the TV remote.
> **SISTER:** Hey, screen face! Over here!

**0:58 · INT. LIVING ROOM** *(red alarm light pulsing)*
The third Collector clamps a claw on Mason's arm. His phone flashes red: **COLLECTING…**, with a progress bar filling. *BEEP. BEEP.*
> The third one grabbed my arm. My phone started beeping.

**1:01 · INT. DOORWAY** *(backlit, slow motion)*
GRANDMA walks in through the blown-open door, cane in hand, glasses glinting, completely unbothered. A brass and choir sting.
> Then Grandma walked in.

**1:03 · INT. LIVING ROOM**
The Collector turns to her and leans down. She slaps her phone, which reads **31y 0d**, flat onto his screen. *SLAP!*
> **GRANDMA:** Overdue? Put it on my tab.

**1:05 · SPLIT SCREEN — TWO PHONES**
GRANDMA's phone drains from **31y** to **00:00:30**. Green light streams down into MASON's phone, which climbs from **0y** to **31y**.
> Her numbers went down. Mine went up.

**1:07 · INT. LIVING ROOM**
The Collector's screen turns green: a checkmark and **ACCOUNT SETTLED**. He straightens his tie and walks out. The other two limp after him.
> His screen said, account settled. And he walked away.

---

### ACT FIVE — 61 YEARS

**1:10 · INT. LIVING ROOM**
Mom, Dad and Sister step forward and hold their phones up to Mason.
> Then Mom, Dad and my sister held up their phones too.

**1:13 · MASON'S POINT OF VIEW**
Their phones each drop ten years. Green light streams into Mason's phone, which climbs to **61y**.
> **MOM:** Take ten years each. We've got plenty.

**1:16 · INT. LIVING ROOM — DAWN** *(warm light)*
Dad is stuck upside-down in the broken couch with only his legs kicking. Cut to Grandma in her armchair, laughing her head off. Her phone ticks down from **00:00:30**.
> Grandma had thirty seconds left. She spent them laughing at Dad, stuck in the couch.

**1:20 · EXT. PORCH — SUNRISE**
Mason looks at his phone, soft-eyed: **61y 0d**, ticking.
> My phone says sixty one years now.

**1:23 · INT. DINING ROOM — DAY**
The family eats breakfast. At the head of the table is an empty chair, with Grandma's cane leaning on it and her glasses on the plate. The camera drifts closer, and GRANDMA's ghost fades in: glowing, see-through, smiling at them. Mason smiles back. She **winks**. *Ting.*
> Most of them are hers.

**1:25 · FADE TO BLACK** *(low boom)*
**DEATH COUNTDOWN**

*END*
